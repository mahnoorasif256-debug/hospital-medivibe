Our Legacy & Story

Founded in 2001, MediCare Hospital began with a simple vision — to provide world-class healthcare that is accessible, compassionate, and affordable for everyone. What started as a small 50-bed facility has grown into a premier multi-specialty hospital serving thousands of patients annually.

Over the past two decades, we have continuously evolved with advancements in medical science, investing in cutting-edge technology and attracting some of the finest medical professionals in the country. Our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in healthcare.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver exceptional healthcare services with compassion, integrity, and innovation, ensuring every patient receives personalized care of the highest standard.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a globally recognized healthcare institution that sets benchmarks in medical excellence, research, and patient-centered care.

Cardiology Department

Our cardiology department is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic and interventional facilities. We offer comprehensive cardiac care including echocardiography, stress testing, cardiac catheterization, angioplasty, and heart surgery. Our team of experienced cardiologists and cardiac surgeons work collaboratively to provide personalized treatment plans for each patient.

We specialize in preventive cardiology, helping patients reduce their risk of heart disease through lifestyle modifications, medication management, and regular monitoring.

  • Echocardiography & Stress Testing
  • Cardiac Catheterization
  • Angioplasty & Stenting
  • Heart Surgery & Bypass
  • Preventive Cardiology
  • Cardiac Rehabilitation
🧠

Neurology Department

The neurology department offers comprehensive care for disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system. Our expert neurologists utilize advanced diagnostic tools including MRI, CT scans, EEG, and nerve conduction studies to accurately diagnose and treat neurological conditions.

We provide specialized care for stroke, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's, and various neuromuscular disorders.

  • Stroke Management
  • Epilepsy Treatment
  • Movement Disorders
  • Headache Management
  • Neuropsychological Testing
  • Neuromuscular Care
🦷

Dental Care Department

Our dental department provides comprehensive oral healthcare services for patients of all ages. From routine cleanings and fillings to advanced cosmetic dentistry and oral surgery, our skilled dentists ensure a comfortable and pain-free experience.

We use modern dental technology including digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and laser dentistry to deliver precise and efficient treatments.

  • General Dentistry & Cleanings
  • Cosmetic Dentistry
  • Root Canal Treatment
  • Dental Implants
  • Orthodontics
  • Oral Surgery
🦴

Orthopedics Department

Our orthopedics department offers comprehensive care for musculoskeletal conditions, including bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and muscles. Our orthopedic surgeons specialize in both surgical and non-surgical treatments to restore mobility and reduce pain.

We provide expert care for sports injuries, arthritis, fractures, joint replacements, and spinal disorders using the latest minimally invasive techniques.

  • Joint Replacement Surgery
  • Sports Medicine
  • Spine Surgery
  • Fracture Care
  • Arthroscopic Surgery
  • Physical Therapy
👶

Pediatrics Department

Our pediatrics department is dedicated to the health and well-being of infants, children, and adolescents. We provide comprehensive medical care in a warm, child-friendly environment designed to put young patients at ease.

Our pediatricians are experienced in managing a wide range of childhood conditions, from common illnesses to complex medical needs, ensuring every child receives the best possible care.

  • Well-Child Checkups
  • Vaccinations & Immunizations
  • Childhood Illness Management
  • Growth & Development Monitoring
  • Pediatric Nutrition
  • Adolescent Medicine
👁️

Ophthalmology Department

Our ophthalmology department offers complete eye care services, from routine vision examinations to advanced surgical procedures. Our experienced ophthalmologists use cutting-edge technology to diagnose and treat a wide range of eye conditions.

We provide specialized care for cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and refractive errors.

  • Comprehensive Eye Exams
  • Cataract Surgery
  • Glaucoma Management
  • LASIK & Refractive Surgery
  • Diabetic Eye Care
  • Pediatric Ophthalmology
